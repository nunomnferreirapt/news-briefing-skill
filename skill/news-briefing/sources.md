# Sources

An example table, built for a reader in the Netherlands who follows Portugal too. Replace the country blocks with your own; keep the columns.

The table the briefing uses to label coverage: what kind of outlet each source is and where it is from. You edit it; the skill adds a row when it uses a source that is missing and marks it `added by Claude, date`.

- **Kind** is one of: public broadcaster, wire agency, newspaper, business press, tech press, digest, state media, government or institution, company, research group.
- **Lean** is filled only where it is widely established; a blank means unknown or not useful, not neutral. It is never shown as a score.
- **Access** says whether pages can be read in a run: `open`, `paywall` (headline and lead only), `blocked` (refuses automated reading).

## Worth reading

The voices behind the "Worth reading" section (and the "Career reading" block, when settings has a search block). Essays only, free and readable whole; the Notes say what counts and where to read it. These five are an example: the first run asks whether to keep them, and you add or remove rows yourself. The skill never adds or drops a voice on its own. `id` is what the edition calls the voice (`reads-<id>` as a tag).
