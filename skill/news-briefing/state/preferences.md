## How the counts steer the next edition

Engaged tags and angles get the fuller treatment and first claim on borderline slots; cooling ones get a shorter card; skipped ones go to a one-line brief, unless the story is one of the day's major ones. The briefing never becomes a bubble: a major new story always runs.
